## Direction contract
THESIS: Liquid Glass whose light is the agent: a glass tile lit from inside is a working agent, a dashed outline is an idle one. Merges the author's chosen Lantern Room metaphor with Liquid Glass material. Replaces the Departure Board, which the author rejected, and refuses a glow-orb dashboard.

Lit lanterns have an inner glow (#f1d9a6) and cast light downward (offset, never a zero-offset halo). One vermilion seal (#d4463a) is the only accent: logo, active marker, last-active mark. Cream light text (#f3e9d2); calm near-solid surfaces for dense content. One family, Onest.
STORY: The visitor sees which agents are working at a glance, understands Nebula is a control center for LLM agents, and signs up.
